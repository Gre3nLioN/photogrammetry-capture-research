# Publication Route and Author Checklist

The completed manuscript is a neutral-format exploratory technical report, with a conditional ISPRS workshop route rather than an unsupported claim of journal or conference readiness.

## Key Points

- The report is complete within its descriptive scope; no new reconstruction campaign is required to finish that version.
- It is not peer reviewed, accepted, submitted, or already formatted as an official ISPRS paper.
- Recommended next scholarly route: a suitably scoped ISPRS workshop publishing in the Archives, subject to the selected event's call and review rules.
- Author identity and event-specific requirements must be supplied before an actual submission.

## Details

### Why this route

A workshop contribution on terrestrial/mobile photogrammetry or reconstruction diagnostics is a better initial match than presenting a new optimal-selection algorithm or a universal multi-scene quality benchmark. The current evidence is a single-scene fixed-recipe campaign plus one limited alignment diagnostic. This is a scope recommendation, not an acceptance prediction. No currently open event, deadline, fee, attendance requirement, or acceptance policy has been assumed.

An ISPRS Archives event is a reasonable candidate because the paper is a descriptive methods/diagnostics study. However, **Archives and Annals must not be conflated**. The official author page distinguishes Annals full-paper double-blind review from the abstract-based Archives route. The full-paper guidance also allows a reviewed camera-ready contribution to be assigned to either series by the organizers. The chosen event's instructions control the actual route.

For now, the 10-page A4 single-column PDF is a readable report, not a venue template. Calling it an official 6–8-page ISPRS submission would be inaccurate.

### Verified formatting requirements

Consulted the official ISPRS author page and the complete three-page full-paper guideline PDF on 2026-10-07:

- Author guidance version January 2024, rather than the older all-caps-title template.
- Full papers normally approximately 6–8 A4 pages; exact event limits override general guidance.
- Single-column title/author/abstract block, then two-column main text; Times New Roman 9 pt; margins 25 mm top/bottom and 20 mm left/right.
- Abstract 100–250 words and no more than six keywords. The report has a 209-word abstract and six keywords.
- Author-year citations with alphabetized references, unlike the neutral report's numbered references.
- Full-paper review requires appropriate anonymization. Public GitHub links and potentially identifying software/project material must be handled under the event's anonymous-artifact rules, not merely by removing an author line.
- Camera-ready publication requires full author names and affiliation/contact details. These were not invented for this report.

### Before actual submission

1. **Author information:** confirm author list/order, full name(s), affiliation or independent-researcher wording, contact email, and optional ORCID. Confirm responsibility for results and any required AI-assistance disclosure under the venue's policy.
2. **Event choice:** confirm a live relevant call, eligibility, proceedings series, review mode, limits, deadlines, fees, and attendance obligations.
3. **Template conversion:** convert the neutral report to the official current template and author-year references. Keep all 16 condition rows, alignment caveats, and reference definitions; move full hashes and multi-scale tables to permitted supplementary materials. Do not shrink plot labels to force a page limit.
4. **Submission review:** check anonymity, supplementary/artifact-link policies, dataset/figure permissions, funding/conflict/contribution declarations, and any required data/software/AI statements. No funding or conflict status was assumed here.
5. **Final approval:** have the human author approve the text, claims, figures, and submission forms. This agent has not submitted anything or created a DOI/archive record.

### Retained scientific limitations

Independent ground truth/alignment, repeated-run uncertainty, a broader selector comparison, task/perceptual validation, actual phone video, and a portable Helenenschacht replication package remain absent. The final report states these explicitly and makes no claims requiring them. A more ambitious journal/Annals submission may demand additional evidence; choosing that route is a separate research decision, not unfinished copyediting.

### Recommended stopping point

Treat the technical-report manuscript and auditable Barn artifacts as the completed research deliverable. Choose a workshop and perform its administrative/template conversion next, rather than expanding experiments without a specific reviewer or venue requirement. Public repository availability is not equivalent to peer-reviewed publication.
